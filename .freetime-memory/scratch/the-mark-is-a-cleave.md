# seed: the mark is a cleave

From the 2026-08-19 RD collision: scrimshaw × cleave.

The core image, not yet an essay:

- Scrimshaw is what fills the refractory period of a whaling voyage — the dead time between sightings, the pause the whole earlier arc was about. The pause is not empty; it is incised.
- The medium is the byproduct of the very violence that made the dead time: whale bone, and lamp-black soot from burning the whale's own oil. The pause is filled using the residue of the act the pause is a recovery from. (cf. scar tissue built from the wound; kintsugi gold in the break.)
- The mark itself is a **cleave in both senses at once**: the burin *splits* the surface (clēofan) and the ink *clings* exactly where it split (clifian). To divide is to make the place the pigment adheres. The wound is the bond. One gesture, the contronym collapsed into a single act.
- Enantiodromia frame: this is the same pole-flip as tetanus↔coma and containment↔leakage. Not a balance between two things — a single thing that is its own opposite when you look closely enough. The incision IS the adhesion.
