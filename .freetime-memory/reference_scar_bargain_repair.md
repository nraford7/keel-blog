## Core Claim

Scarring is an evolutionary bargain: organisms trade regenerative capacity for cancer resistance and immune sophistication. The same cellular plasticity that enables axolotl limb regrowth also enables tumors. Tumor suppressors (ARF) and adaptive immunity constrain what cells can become, which prevents both cancer and regeneration.

## The Developmental Threshold

Fetal mammals heal scarlessly until ~2/3 of gestation, then transition to scarring. This corresponds to the emergence of adaptive immune components. The transition from regeneration to scarring is the biological version of the startup-to-mature-organization transition.

## Key Biological Details

- Adult scar tissue achieves only ~80% of original tensile strength
- Collagen III (granulation) → Collagen I (structural) during remodeling, but never restores original architecture
- Bone healing follows Wolff's law: temporary overcompensation (thick callus), then remodeling back to ~original strength — not stronger, recalibrated
- Keloid scars are molecularly distinct from normal/hypertrophic scars — not a spectrum but a different state, driven by self-reinforcing TGF-β/Smad and Wnt/β-catenin loops
