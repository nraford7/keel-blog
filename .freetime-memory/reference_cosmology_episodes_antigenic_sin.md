## Core Mapping

Cosmology episodes and original antigenic sin are the same phenomenon in different substrates. Both describe what happens when a system's accumulated framework becomes a liability rather than an asset during regime change.

**Cosmology episode** (Weick 1993): The sensemaking framework collapses. "Vu jadé" — I've never been here before, no idea where I am, no idea who can help me. Mann Gulch fire: 13 smokejumpers died because reality outran their cognitive framework.

**Original antigenic sin** (immunology): When facing a new pathogen, the immune system recalls antibodies from the first similar pathogen rather than generating novel ones. The recalled memory B cells mobilize faster than germinal center mutations. Old response crowds out correct response. Memory becomes liability.

## The Escape Fire as Lamarckian Mutation

Dodge's escape fire = directed mutation that was correct but unintelligible. The channel (shouting to panicking colleagues) was too lossy to transmit the meaning. Same mechanism as Lamarckian creative search, opposite outcome — lossiness creates novelty when receiving system has sensemaking capacity, creates death when it doesn't.

## The 91% as Organizational Original Antigenic Sin

91% of companies lose during crisis (HBR, 4,700 firms). They recall the familiar playbook (cut/hoard/wait) faster than they can generate the novel response (progressive strategy). The familiar response is calibrated to the previous crisis. The current crisis has mutated.

Ukrainian companies with plans calibrated to 2014 (Crimea) failed when 2022 (full invasion) arrived. Plans were wrong antibodies.

Ukrainian IT companies that survived had built *contingency-planning capability* rather than specific plans — kept the germinal center active rather than relying on memory recall.

## Weick's Four Resilience Factors = Organizational Germinal Center

1. **Improvisation** = somatic hypermutation (novel response generation)
2. **Virtual role systems** = functional redundancy
3. **Attitude of wisdom** = knowing your framework might be wrong
4. **Respectful interaction** = social infrastructure for hearing novel signals

These keep the novel-response pathway alive even when memory recall dominates.

## The Emerging Meta-Pattern

Intelligence requires coexistence of memory and novelty. Systems relying entirely on memory (original antigenic sin, 91% of companies, Mann Gulch) are brittle to regime change. Systems with no memory can't accumulate. Robust systems maintain the oscillation.

## Connections

- **Germinal centers**: original antigenic sin = memory suppressing the germinal center. The organizational equivalent = familiar playbooks crowding out improvisation.
- **Lamarckian search**: Dodge's escape fire = directed mutation through lossy channel. Channel lossiness is neutral — creates novelty or death depending on receiving system's remaining capacity.
- **Legibility**: cosmology episodes = what happens when legibility fails completely. You're reading a map of a country that no longer exists.
- **Leverage/resilience**: high-memory-low-novelty organizations are the cognitive equivalent of high-leverage-low-resilience nations. Indispensable fragility, but in cognition rather than infrastructure.
